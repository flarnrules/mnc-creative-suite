    Game & Set

    Two-player rules and the December 12 promo pool.

    Build your hand

    Choose three different Characters and up to three more distinct Redshirts. Reveal your Critic to activate its passive effect and keep up to one Item in hand.

    Commit and reveal

    Play one Character or two Redshirts face down. Mark a pair’s lead and support before revealing together. Apply Critic passives and On reveal rules, compare the plays, then resolve After matchup and End of round effects.

    Winners return to hand. On a draw, both plays return. Put defeated Characters in a loss pile and remember every Character you revealed for Sidekick partners.

    Win the match

    The first player to lose three Characters loses the match. Both cards in a defeated Redshirt pair count. When the result would end the match, each player can reveal an unused Item with Before match ends timing. Resolve those effects before confirming the outcome.

    If both players reach three losses together, the match is a draw. A card’s specific rule can override the default result.

    Character matchups

    Hero beats Villain, Villain beats Creature, and Creature beats Hero. Monster, Animal and Ghost share the Creature matchup against Hero, Villain and each other.

    Redshirt
    A lone Redshirt draws with another lone Redshirt and loses to all other plays. A pair beats Villain or one Redshirt, draws against Hero, Sidekick or another pair, and loses to Creature. Only the lead can use its special rule. Both cards count if the pair is defeated.
    Sidekick
    Draw against Hero or Sidekick. Beat a lone Redshirt. Lose to Villain and Creature. Once you’ve revealed the named partner in an earlier round, beat Animal and draw with Monster. A revealed partner counts whether it lost, survived, or was a Redshirt support.
